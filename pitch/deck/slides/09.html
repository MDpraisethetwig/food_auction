Too Good To Go and Flashfood sell fixed discounts. We price live, on the exact meal. Our edge is local density and data on what sells, and when. We also build AI in: photo to listing automates the food photo workflow, voice search curates meals around price, diet and allergies, and the business dashboard uses AI to summarize surplus and offers Boosts that bump a listing to the top of the feed and notify users. Our target is to double the surplus a business sells, which the pilot will measure.
