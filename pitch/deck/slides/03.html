There are two problems. Restaurants make food that goes unsold or gets cancelled, and it ends up in the bin. Students want meals they can afford. We checked. 10 local restaurants told us food waste is a real problem for them, and 50 McMaster students said they'd buy cheaper food. Full4Less connects the two.
