On one side, restaurants prepare food that sometimes goes unsold or gets cancelled, and it ends up in the garbage. On the other side, students are looking for affordable meals. Full4Less connects these two problems. We checked this ourselves. Ten local restaurants told us they throw away food waste and that it's a problem, and 50 McMaster students said they'd be willing to purchase cheaper food.
