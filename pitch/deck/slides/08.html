Illustrative scenario; assumes 5 eligible meals per business per day, average C$10 sale, 15% commission on transaction value and 300 operating days. Counts include businesses that may not qualify or participate.

We sized this bottom-up. Five eligible meals a day, an average C$10 sale, 15% commission and 300 operating days gives C$2,250 per business per year. Our initial pilot is five businesses, so C$11,250. Apply the same figure to Hamilton's 886 small food and beverage businesses and you get C$1.99 million. Across Ontario's 29,885 employer establishments, C$67.24 million. All of that is annual commission revenue. We go Hamilton first: McMaster students, then Westdale, Ainslie Wood and downtown, with Toronto next. I want to be plain: these numbers are illustrative until the pilot measures real supply, and the counts include businesses that may not qualify or participate.
