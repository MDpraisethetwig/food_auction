Estimates. Per partner: about 6 surplus items (~C$4) and 1 cancelled order (~C$8) a day over 300 days, at 20% commission plus a C$0.50 buyer fee. Assumes every food establishment has end-of-day surplus; the North America outlet count is a rough estimate. Excludes subscriptions and boosts.

We sized this bottom-up, one partner at a time. Surplus items are the core of the supply: we assume a typical partner lists about six surplus items a day, plus about one cancelled order. At our 20% commission plus the 50-cent buyer fee, that's about C$9.90 a day, or roughly C$2,970 per partner per year. Our obtainable market is Hamilton: 75 to 125 partners in the first two years, out of 886 small food and beverage businesses, so about C$220 to 370 thousand a year. Our serviceable market is Ontario. Almost every food business has something left at the end of the day, so all 29,885 food establishments qualify. That's about C$89 million a year. Our total market is North America, roughly a million food outlets across Canada and the U.S., about C$3 billion a year. To be plain, these are estimates until the pilot measures how many surplus items each partner really lists, and they don't include subscriptions or boosts.
