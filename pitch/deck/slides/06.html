Here's how it works. Staff snap a photo of unsold food or a cancelled order. Our AI drafts the listing from the photo and a quick voice note, and staff confirm the allergens themselves, never the AI. Nearby customers bid live, or hit Buy Now. When the timer ends, the winner is charged automatically and picks up by scanning a QR code. Miss your pickup, and staff check the food is still safe, then put it back up for auction. Unlike a surprise bag, you see the exact meal, and demand sets the price. The prototype runs today in test mode.
