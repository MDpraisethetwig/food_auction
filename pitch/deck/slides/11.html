We're not starting with a big marketing campaign. We're starting in one city, Hamilton, and in one spot inside it. Our first channel is student ambassadors on McMaster campus. People talking to people. That's where our effort goes first. From campus it spreads to the neighbourhoods right beside it, Westdale and Ainslie Wood. Think of it as pings from one pin, not a national launch. Around that we add social media, restaurant partnerships, and posters in the restaurants themselves, so people meet Full4Less where they already eat.
