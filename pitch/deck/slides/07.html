We're B2B2C: restaurants list, customers buy, and we earn 15% of each completed sale. A restaurant pays nothing until something sells. Take one illustrative $10 meal, in Canadian dollars like everything on this slide. The restaurant keeps $8.50 and our commission is $1.50. Card processing takes about 59 cents on our estimate, which leaves about 91 cents per meal before fixed costs. Running costs stay small: Expo's free tier, Apple at about C$139 a year, hosting and database at about C$63 a month, and AI at about C$3 per 1,000 listings. That's about C$78 a month plus payment fees. To be plain, these numbers are illustrative until the pilot measures real supply.
