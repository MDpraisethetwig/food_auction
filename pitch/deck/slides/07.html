Who pays? Both sides, a little. On a 10 dollar meal, the restaurant keeps 8 dollars, and we take a 20 percent commission plus a 50 cent buyer fee. That makes sense because the alternative for the restaurant is zero. After card fees we keep about 1.90. Running costs at pilot scale are about 85 to 105 dollars a month, including Gemini at 10 to 30 dollars for photo, voice and text. As volume grows, we add subscriptions and boosts that don't pay the card fee.
