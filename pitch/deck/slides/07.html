We earn on both sides of every sale. Restaurants pay nothing until something sells, and every sale earns us at least 75 cents. Take one illustrative $10 meal, in Canadian dollars. The buyer pays $10.50, the restaurant keeps $8.00, and we take $2.50: a 20% commission plus a 50-cent buyer fee. The card fee is about 60 cents on our estimate, which leaves about $1.90 per meal before fixed costs. Here's what it costs to run at pilot scale, about ten restaurants and 2,100 listings a month. Gemini reads the photo and the voice note and writes the listing, for about 10 to 30 dollars a month depending on the model. Hosting for our API, database and restaurant site is about 63 dollars. The app stores work out to about 12 dollars a month: Apple charges 99 US dollars a year, Google 25 once. Expo builds are free at this size. That's roughly 85 to 105 dollars a month plus card fees. Two costs come later: Stripe payouts to restaurants, about five dollars per restaurant a month once payouts are live, and liability insurance, which we're getting quoted. As volume grows we add wallet credits, F4L+ for buyers, Restaurant Pro and paid boosts. These numbers are illustrative until the pilot measures real supply.
