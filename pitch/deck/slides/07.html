We earn on both sides of every sale. Restaurants pay nothing until something sells, and every sale earns us at least 75 cents. Take one illustrative $10 meal, in Canadian dollars. The buyer pays $10.50, the restaurant keeps $8.00, and we take $2.50: a 20% commission plus a 50-cent buyer fee. The card fee is about 60 cents on our estimate, which leaves about $1.90 per meal before fixed costs, roughly double a plain 15% commission. As volume grows we add more: wallet credits, so we pay one card fee per top-up instead of per meal; F4L+ for buyers at $4.99 a month; Restaurant Pro at $19 a month with a lower 12% commission and waste analytics; and paid boosts. None of those depend on the per-meal card fee. Running costs stay small, about C$75 a month plus AI and card fees. To be plain, these numbers are illustrative until the pilot measures real supply and what restaurants and students accept.
